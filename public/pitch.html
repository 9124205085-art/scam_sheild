HACKDAY 1.0 · Tech for a better tomorrow

Scam Shield

An AI scam checker that explains itself, in your language.

01 · Problem

Victims understand the scam after the money is gone.

Fake KYC, digital-arrest calls, parcel fees, job offers, UPI collect requests. Filters fail silently. Almost none explain the danger in Hindi, Tamil, or the language a parent actually reads.

02 · Solution

Check before you tap, pay, or share an OTP.

  • Paste a message, screenshot, link, or number
  • Verdict + confidence in seconds
  • Red flags highlighted in the original text
  • Audio explanation + next steps

03 · Hybrid engine

Rules first. AI second. Scores on screen.

Lookalike domains, shorteners, punycode, urgency, OTP, AnyDesk. Then an LLM reasons only on masked text. Judges see rules: 78 · AI: 90 — not a wrapped chatbot.

04 · Family Shield

Protection you can forward to your parents.

One tap builds a warning card and WhatsApp text in their language. The viral loop is care, not clicks.

05 · After the damage

“I already clicked or paid.”

First-hour checklist: freeze with the bank, rotate PINs, report. India defaults to 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in. Helplines are configurable by country. Phones and OTPs never leave the browser unmasked.

06 · Impact

Any phone. No install. A habit of checking first.

Built for first-time internet users, elders, and students. Next: a WhatsApp bot, more languages, and telecom feeds. Until then — check before you pay.
